That framing resonates with how I approach technology. At every company I have built, the wins that mattered most came not from automating what already existed but from rearchitecting the system so the old bottlenecks simply disappeared. Relativity is doing that at a scale and stakes level that is unmatched.

Terran R is real and gaining momentum, but the Data & AI organization is still early enough that the person in this seat will define the strategy, the architecture, and the culture. I have built engineering organizations from zero multiple times, through Y Combinator, through a successful exit, and through scaling enterprise platforms. I know what it looks like to set technical direction when there is no playbook, and I find that phase of company building the most energizing. The opportunity to do that at Relativity, where the decisions I make will directly shape how autonomous factories and launch systems operate, is not something I am willing to pass up.

Third, the leadership transition under Eric Schmidt signals exactly the kind of disciplined, engineering-driven scaling that matches how I operate. My career has been defined by bringing technical rigor to ambitious, uncharted problems, whether that was building HIPAA-compliant AI systems in emergency medicine, shipping a health platform through a pandemic at YC, or architecting content authentication pipelines that need to be both fast and trustworthy. Schmidt's track record of scaling world-class technology organizations tells me Relativity is entering a phase where operational excellence and bold technical ambition will need to coexist, and that is the environment where I do my best work.

Beyond all of that, I genuinely believe that expanding humanity's access to space is one of the most important problems of our generation. I have spent my career building technology that serves real, high-stakes needs. Relativity is the place where data engineering, AI, and distributed systems are not just business tools but the foundation of something that will outlast all of us. That matters to me.
