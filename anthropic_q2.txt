Why Anthropic?

I want to work at Anthropic because I believe the company is asking the right question at the right time: how do we build AI that is genuinely safe, reliable, and beneficial, and then put it into the hands of people and organizations in a way that actually works? That question sits at the center of everything I have spent my career doing, and Anthropic is the only company I see pursuing it with both the technical rigor and the moral seriousness it demands.

As a serial CTO and Y Combinator founder who has built AI-powered platforms from zero, I have seen firsthand how the gap between what AI can do in a research setting and what it actually delivers in production is where trust is made or broken. At CopyCatch.AI, I architect LLM-powered pipelines for content authentication, and the challenges I navigate daily - evaluation design, reliability at scale, responsible deployment - are the same challenges Anthropic is solving at the frontier. At FlutterFlow, I was the technical partner guiding enterprise customers through complex platform adoptions, and I learned that the most impactful work happens when you deeply understand both the technology and the customer's real-world constraints. That intersection - frontier AI capability meets enterprise reality - is exactly where the Solutions Architect role lives.

What sets Anthropic apart for me is the commitment to safety as a first principle, not an afterthought. I have built systems under HIPAA compliance at Henry Ford Health System and in high-stakes startup environments through Y Combinator, so I understand what it means to ship technology where the consequences of getting it wrong are serious. Anthropic's approach to building interpretable, steerable AI aligns with how I have always built: with intention, rigor, and accountability. I want to be part of the team that helps the world adopt AI responsibly, and I believe Anthropic is where that work matters most.
